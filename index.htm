Offline-First App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Summary: Trading Journal is built as an offline-first mobile application. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your trades, financial metrics, account details, or personal data to any external server or third-party entity.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Trading Journal application (Package: com.hjstudio.tradingjournal.tjrn) developed by HJ Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our handling practices regarding user data and our dedication to safeguarding your privacy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our fundamental principle is user privacy and data sovereignty:

  • Local Storage: All trading data (including trade entries, lot sizes, instruments, PnL numbers, notes, and emotional/psychology logs) is stored strictly on your local device using an encrypted and sandboxed local SQLite/Room database.
  • No Cloud Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register, or provide personal credentials (like passwords, phone numbers, or emails) to use the application.
  • No Financial Execution or Brokerage Access: The app does not connect to brokerage APIs (e.g., MetaTrader, Binance, Zerodha, etc.), does not process bank deposits or withdrawals, and does not execute real monetary transactions.

3. Device Permissions

The application only accesses minimum required device capabilities strictly necessary for core features:

  • Storage / Media: If you attach chart screenshots to a trade log, the app accesses local storage solely to load your chosen image into the local record. These screenshots never leave your device.
  • Notifications (Optional): If scheduled trade reminders or daily journaling alerts are enabled, the app uses standard local Android notifications.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking networks, or commercial analytics frameworks that harvest or sell behavioral data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • You can edit or delete any individual trade, psychology record, or trading account at any time within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the application's storage data in Android Settings will permanently remove all stored trades and logs from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for users aged 18 and older who engage in financial education and personal trading analysis. We do not knowingly collect or solicit information from anyone under the age of 18.

7. Security

We leverage standard Android sandboxing and platform security mechanisms to prevent unauthorized applications from accessing your local app database. However, users are encouraged to secure their physical devices with screen locks and biometric authentication.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.
